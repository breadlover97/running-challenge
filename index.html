Data Source & Validation

Data comes from the Strava API. Garmin and Apple Watch runs count after they sync into Strava. Manual activities are excluded unless the organiser explicitly allows them for a participant.

This site shows mileage, run counts, teams, and Strava validation links. It does not display GPS maps, exact start or end locations, heart rate, cadence, power, or coordinates.
