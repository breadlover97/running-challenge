# Participant Setup Guide

This challenge uses Strava as the source of truth. Runs recorded on Garmin, Apple Watch, or the Strava app count only after they appear in Strava as running activities.

## Join The Challenge

1. Open [https://breadlover97.github.io/running-challenge/](https://breadlover97.github.io/running-challenge/).
2. Tap **Sign in with Strava**.
3. Log in to Strava.
4. Approve the challenge app once.
5. Enter your display name.
6. Choose **Team A** or **Team B**.
7. Submit the join form.

You do not need to use terminal, copy a code, or create any GitHub/Cloudflare account.

## Garmin Users

1. Open Garmin Connect.
2. Go to connected apps or account settings.
3. Connect Garmin Connect to Strava.
4. Confirm future Garmin activities automatically sync to Strava.
5. Open Strava and make sure your run appears as a `Run`.

## Apple Watch Users

1. Open the Strava app.
2. Connect Apple Health to Strava.
3. Allow Strava to read workout data.
4. Import or sync Apple Watch runs into Strava.
5. Open Strava and make sure your run appears as a `Run`.

## What Counts

- Only Strava `Run` activities count.
- Only activities dated from **4 May 2026** through **31 December 2026** count.
- The challenge uses the activity date, not the upload date.
- A run inside the challenge period can be uploaded later and still count.
- A run before the challenge period does not count even if uploaded during the challenge.
- Manual activities are excluded by default.

## Consent And Privacy

By joining, you agree that:

- Your display name, team, profile photo or initials, total distance, run count, and recent activity links may appear on the website.
- Your daily mileage may be posted in the Telegram group.
- GPS maps, exact start/end locations, coordinates, heart rate, cadence, and power will not be displayed.
- You can leave by asking the organiser to remove you from the challenge.

## Troubleshooting

My Garmin run is not showing on Strava:

- Check that Garmin Connect is linked to Strava.
- Confirm the activity uploaded successfully in Garmin Connect.
- Wait a few minutes and refresh Strava.
- Confirm the activity type is `Run`.

My Apple Watch run is not showing on Strava:

- Check Strava's Apple Health permissions.
- Try importing the workout from Strava's Apple Health import screen.
- Confirm the activity type is `Run`.

My run is private:

- Private activities may not be returned unless you approved the required Strava permission.
- Ask the organiser if the run still does not appear after the next sync.

My run is recorded as walk, hike, or workout:

- Edit the sport type in Strava if appropriate.
- It will count after the next sync if the activity date is inside the challenge period.

My mileage is wrong:

- Compare against the distance shown in Strava.
- The challenge uses Strava distance, not Garmin Connect or Apple Fitness distance.
- Ask the organiser to check whether the run was excluded as manual, non-run, private, or outside the challenge dates.
